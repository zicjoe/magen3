# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Magen3 community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels. Maintainers will review reports fairly and confidentially and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
